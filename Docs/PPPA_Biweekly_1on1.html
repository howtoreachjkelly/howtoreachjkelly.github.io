PPPA — Biweekly 1:1

Progress · Problems · Priorities · Actions

Keep this lightweight. The value is in the cadence and the follow-through, not the format — a few concrete lines per section beat an exhaustive log every time. Click into any field to edit directly.

Progress

What moved forward since last time? Focus on outcomes, not activity — what's actually done or closer to done.

Problems

What's stuck, at risk, or harder than expected? Naming a problem here is not a performance mark against anyone — it's the point of the meeting.

Priorities

What matters most for the next two weeks? Rank if more than two or three things — priorities that aren't ranked aren't really priorities.

Actions

Who owns what, by when? Every action needs a name and a date — an action with no owner is just a hope.

ActionOwnerDue

Carry unresolved Problems and open Actions forward into the next meeting automatically — don't let them quietly drop. Actions should get simpler over time, not more elaborate; if this template starts feeling heavy, cut it back rather than adding more structure to it.
